Privacy Policy

MB Channel Manager · Effective 23 November 2026

MB Channel Manager ("the app") is a private desktop application used only by its owner to manage YouTube channels the owner operates. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app may access:

  • Basic channel information (channel name and ID)
  • Video information on the owner's channels (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy status, scheduled publish times and localizations)
  • Video and thumbnail files that the owner uploads through the app

The app does not access the data of any other YouTube user, and does not collect viewers' personal information.

3. How the data is used

Data is used only to upload, schedule, update and translate videos on the owner's own channels, and to keep the owner's internal production records up to date. Video titles and descriptions may be sent to Google's Gemini API to generate translations, which are then written back to the same videos.

4. Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never published or sent to any server other than Google's. Video metadata may be kept in the owner's private Google Sheets for production tracking.

5. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share data with third parties. Data is sent only to Google services (YouTube Data API, Google Sheets and Gemini API) to carry out the functions described above.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, locally stored tokens stop working and can be deleted.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
